## Description

ClipIQ remembers everything you copy — so you never lose a link, command, or address again.

📋 YOUR CLIPBOARD, FINALLY SEARCHABLE
Every copy lands in ClipIQ automatically — links, code, emails, colors, text. Instant fuzzy search finds the right clip in milliseconds. Pin favorites, mark sensitive clips private (they vanish after pasting), and keep hundreds of items without slowing down.

🧠 SMART CARDS
Links, emails, colors, code, and commands are recognized automatically and shown as clean, typed cards — a color chip, the domain of a URL, an email badge. No wall of identical text.

✨ PRO — FREE, UNLOCKED BY SUPPORTING CLIPIQ (OPTIONAL)
Everything works without an account. Optionally, unlock Pro features free by sharing a slice of your unused internet bandwidth (with your explicit consent — you choose, and you can opt out any time):
• 💬 AI Chat — a separate premium chat window that knows your recent clips. Ask "what was that curl command?", "summarize this", "rewrite this email politely".
• 🔎 AI Search — describe what you're looking for in plain words; ClipIQ finds the exact clip.
• 🏷️ AI Labels — one tap gives any clip a smart gold label so your history stays organized.
• 👑 Premium look — gold icon & premium UI while Pro is active. Opt out and everything instantly returns to normal.

No ads. No data sold. No credit card. No API keys or configuration — AI features simply work.

⚡ SNIPPETS
Type ;addr + Space in any text field and it expands into your saved address. Snippets work everywhere: forms, editors, chat apps.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything is stored locally on your machine — no accounts, no cloud, no sync required.
• Copies made on blocked sites (your bank, password manager) are never saved — you control the list.
• Private clips burn after pasting.
• Export or wipe your history any time.

⌨️ KEYBOARD FIRST
Ctrl+Shift+V opens ClipIQ anywhere. ↑↓ to navigate, Enter to paste, Ctrl+Enter to ask AI.

WHY CLIPIQ?
Built after studying hundreds of reviews of other clipboard extensions. We engineered around every common complaint: no silent failures (capture health is visible), no data wipes (careful storage + export/backup), no shady permissions creep (minimum permissions, clearly explained), and no performance leaks (large copies are ignored by default).

## Privacy notes for the review (single-permission rationale)
- storage — saves your clip history locally
- clipboardRead/clipboardWrite — the entire point of the extension
- contextMenus — "Search clipboard with ClipIQ" right-click entry
- alarms — clipboard polling + Pro status checks
- offscreen — reads the clipboard from a hidden document (required by Chrome)
- activeTab, scripting — paste the chosen clip into the page you're on
- declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess + <all_urls> — used ONLY by the optional Mellowtel support layer (consensual bandwidth sharing, off by default; users opt in themselves). ClipIQ's core features need no site access.
